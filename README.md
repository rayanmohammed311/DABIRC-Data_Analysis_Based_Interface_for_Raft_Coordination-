WELCOME TO DABIRC!

This program simulates a raft level censor, a function of Project Nerosiya by Team Nerosiya from GEMS New Millennium School Dubai.
This prototype was presented at the AtkinsRealis School of Future Competition at the University of Birmingham, Dubai.

You can simply connect it to your mobile phone, which must have a tilt censor. Then, the streamlit webpage will demonstrate the raft level detector at work. Enjoy!
